1. Reuse the design system's components and tokens; a new component or style needs a recorded reason.
2. Specify every screen in all its states: default, empty, loading, error, success, disabled, and over-long content.
3. Meet accessibility: keyboard operation, focus order, visible focus, labels, contrast, text alternatives, no information by colour alone.
4. Describe responsive behaviour and the supported viewports.
5. Write all user-facing copy, including errors, and keep it consistent with existing wording.
6. Hand off with measurements, tokens, interactions and acceptance checks the developer and tester can verify.
