1. Analysis before design, design before plan, plan before tasks; each stage is signed off by a human before the next starts (large features).
2. List at least two options for every structural choice and record the deciding reason in a decision record.
3. Split work into tasks small enough for one session, each naming its modules and acceptance criteria.
4. Check that the design respects the architecture doc and the rules; call out any rule it would break.
5. List open questions explicitly; do not plan around an unanswered question.
6. Identify cross-module and upstream impact and risks.
